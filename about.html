About MoneyQuest

Everyone deserves to feel capable with money.

MoneyQuest is a nonprofit learning community for young people who are ready to ask better questions, test ideas, and build confidence one decision at a time.

🎯

Our mission

We help young people build real confidence with money by making financial literacy practical, approachable, and exciting. We believe students learn best when they can test decisions, collaborate with peers, and connect money choices to everyday life.

🌱

How we started

MoneyQuest began with a simple frustration: traditional money lessons often felt abstract, passive, or too far removed from how kids actually experience life. We created a nonprofit learning model where students solve problems, build strategies, and work through real-world financial scenarios in a fun, hands-on format.

🛠️

Our method

Each Saturday workshop is built around a challenge that mixes teamwork, creativity, and decision-making. Students learn budgeting, trade-offs, planning, and resource management while they play.

👧

Ages 8+

Our experiences are designed to be inclusive and flexible, giving younger students a clear entry point while also letting older students dig into deeper strategies and more advanced thinking.

🤝

Why it works

Students learn by doing instead of being told. The result is stronger retention, better engagement, and a more natural understanding of how money decisions affect real life.
